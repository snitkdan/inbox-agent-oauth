Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026.

inbox-agent is used only by its owner, on their own Gmail account. It requests access to read, label and archive mail (gmail.modify) and to manage Gmail filters (gmail.settings.basic).

What it does with your data

Message headers and text are read to decide whether a message stays in the inbox. To make that decision, the message content is sent to Anthropic's Claude model through the owner's own Claude account. Copies of messages and the decisions made about them are stored only on the owner's computer.

What it does not do

It does not sell, share, or transfer Gmail data to anyone else, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train models. It never sends or deletes mail. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; local copies are deleted by removing the tool's data folder.
