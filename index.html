inbox-agent

inbox-agent is a personal tool that one person runs on their own computer to triage their own Gmail inbox. It sorts incoming mail, archives newsletters, promotions and notifications under labels, and keeps the rest in the inbox. It maintains Gmail filters for senders the owner has chosen to always archive or always keep.

It is not a service, has no other users, and is not offered to anyone else.
